How does the {{ system }} rating work?
{% if system == 'elo' %}

Everyone starts at 1500. After each match, points are exchanged based on who was expected to win. Beating a stronger player earns more; beating a weaker one earns less.

E = 1 / (1 + 10((R_opp − R_you) / 400)

The K factor controls how fast ratings move. New players use K=40 (first 5 games), then K=24 (games 6–15), then K=20. Bigger wins apply a small multiplier (up to ×1.15). Rematches on the same day are progressively damped.

Full explanation with worked example →

{% else %}

Glicko-2 adds a Rating Deviation (RD) to Elo — it measures how confident we are about a rating. New players have high RD (uncertain), which decreases as they play more.

Players start at 1500 ± 350 RD. A lower RD means the rating is more reliable.
